## Selection rationale

The batch keeps four complete rollout groups together: B3 Refund personas (2), B5 Registration lifecycle (4), B7 Workflow/status (8), and B2 Installments (6). B3, B5, and B7 directly reuse the refund, registration, alert, status, details, and RTL patterns validated by the six-template pilot. B2 fills the remaining six slots with one coherent financial group rather than cherry-picking easy templates; it adds paid-installment invoices, payment reminders, refund tables, observer personas, and conditional fee/discount columns while reusing the approved Commerce shell.

Evidence was checked through targeted `email-cli context` calls, the matching traceability rows, the 20 production templates under `D:\last\rescounts-backend\email\templates\`, their referenced locale packs, and their named sender/call-site evidence. No non-selected production template was loaded for implementation.
